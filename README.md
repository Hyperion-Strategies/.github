## Open source

### NullGrid

**Offline knowledge, an AI that shows its sources, and radios — on hardware you own.**

[![Python 3.11+](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3.11%2B-F3B344?style=flat-square&labelColor=28323C&logo=python&logoColor=white)][nullgrid]
[![License: AGPL-3.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-AGPL--3.0-F3B344?style=flat-square&labelColor=28323C)][nullgrid-license]
[![Runs on Raspberry Pi](https://img.shields.io/badge/Runs_on-Raspberry_Pi-F3B344?style=flat-square&labelColor=28323C&logo=raspberrypi&logoColor=white)][nullgrid]

NullGrid turns a PC, a laptop or a Raspberry Pi into a box that everyone in the house reaches from a phone over Wi-Fi when the internet is gone: a power cut, a hurricane, a cabin, a boat.

- **Answers you can check.** Ask by typing, or out loud in English or Spanish. A local AI searches an offline library (Ready.gov, CDC, MedlinePlus, WikEM, U.S. government survival and first-aid manuals, Wikipedia and more) and shows the exact passage behind every answer.
- **First aid with the AI off.** About forty step-by-step pages: CPR with a metronome, a tourniquet timer, burns, stroke, anaphylaxis.
- **Knows your area.** Offline maps with flood and evacuation zones, NOAA weather-radio alerts and a forecast from a barometer.
- **Built for a bad day.** A situation room that opens when the power goes or the barometer drops, a neighborhood message board over LoRa radio (Meshtastic), and an encrypted vault for IDs, insurance and medical records.
- **Runs on what you have.** Windows, Linux, Docker or a Raspberry Pi, as one small Python program you run yourself. It needs the internet only for setup, and an offline bundle on a USB stick skips even that.

AI models make mistakes, which is why every answer shows its sources.

NullGrid is free under the AGPL-3.0. If the AGPL doesn't fit how you want to ship it, [ask about a commercial license](https://www.hyperion-strat.com/#contact).

## How a project runs

1. **Talk.** A 20-minute scoping call.
2. **Scope.** A fixed scope and fee wherever the shape is clear.
3. **Build.** The principal writes the code, with a demo every week.
4. **Run.** Through launch, stabilization and handoff.

Want a number first? Answer four questions on the [cost estimator](https://www.hyperion-strat.com/estimate) for a ballpark range. Ongoing work runs on month-to-month [retainers](https://www.hyperion-strat.com/retainers): Advisory, Embedded or Fractional CTO.
